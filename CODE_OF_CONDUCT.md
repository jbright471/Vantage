# Code of Conduct

Vantage uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct as its community baseline.

## Our Standard

We want Vantage to be a practical, respectful space for homelab operators, AI engineers, and infrastructure tinkerers.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Be direct, kind, and specific in technical feedback.
- Assume others are operating different hardware, budgets, and risk tolerances.
- Share reproducible details without exposing private network or secret material.
- Keep disagreement focused on design tradeoffs, safety, and maintainability.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, secrets, or network details without consent.
- Dismissing safety concerns around host-level actions, auth, or audit data.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic demands.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
